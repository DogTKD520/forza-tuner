# Alignment
- Camber: Negative camber helps outside tires sit flat on the road during body roll. Start at 0 to -1.0 front and rear. RWD: front slightly more negative. FWD: rear slightly more negative. AWD: balanced or RWD style.
- Toe: Front toe-out sharpens turn-in. Rear toe-in stabilises the rear. Toe scrubs tires and costs top speed. Deviation cases: FWD hesitates on turn-in -> +0.1 front toe-out. RWD snaps on throttle -> -0.1 to -0.2 rear toe-in.
- Front Caster: More straight-line stability and dynamic camber in turns. Start at 6.5-7.0 degrees. Off-road may run lower (2.0) for more travel and less self-centering.
