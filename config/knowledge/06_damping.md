# Damping
Springs control how far the suspension moves. Dampers control how fast.
- Bump stiffness: Controls the speed of suspension compression (hitting a bump, or weight transferring). Stiff bump = fast weight transfer (car reacts faster but skips over bumps). Soft bump = slow weight transfer (car is lazier but compliant).
- Rebound stiffness: Controls the speed of suspension extension (wheel dropping into a dip, or body rolling back to level). Stiff rebound keeps the body from bouncing up too fast.

Balance rules:
- Stiffer front bump -> Understeer on entry
- Stiffer rear bump -> Oversteer on exit / power
- Stiffer front rebound -> Understeer on exit
- Stiffer rear rebound -> Oversteer on entry

Golden ratio: Bump should be roughly 40-50% of Rebound.
