# Balance & Fix-It
Symptom → likely cause → adjustment.

## Understeer Cards
- U1 Entry Understeer (refuses to turn in when braking): Soften front ARB, soften front spring rate, soften front rebound damping, shift brake bias rearward, reduce rear diff decel, increase caster.
- U2 Mid-corner Understeer (steady-state pushing): Soften front ARB, lower front tire pressure, add negative front camber, increase front downforce, lower front ride height.
- U3 Exit Understeer (washes out on power, common on AWD/FWD): Reduce front diff accel, stiffen rear spring rate, reduce rear downforce, stiffen rear bump damping, reduce rear toe-in.
- U4 High-speed Understeer: Increase front downforce, add negative front camber, lower front ride height, reduce rear downforce.

## Oversteer Cards
- O1 Entry Oversteer (rear steps out on lift/trail-brake): Increase rear diff decel, soften rear ARB, shift brake bias forward, soften rear spring rate, stiffen front rebound.
- O2 Mid-corner Oversteer (steady cornering constant throttle): Soften rear ARB, add negative rear camber, increase rear downforce, lower rear ride height.
- O3 Power Oversteer (snap on throttle, RWD): Reduce rear diff accel, soften rear spring rate, soften rear bump damping, increase rear downforce, increase rear toe-in.
- O4 Snap Oversteer (violent sudden): Soften rear ARB, soften rear spring rate, soften rear rebound, soften rear bump, reduce rear diff accel.

## Other Problems
- B1 Nose Dives Under Braking: Stiffen front bump, stiffen front springs, stiffen front rebound.
- B2 Bouncy / Floaty: Stiffen bump (both), stiffen rebound (both), stiffen springs.
- B3 Skitters Over Bumps / Curbs (loses grip suddenly on rough): Soften bump damping, soften springs, soften ARBs, raise ride height.
- B4 Bottoms Out / Scrapes: Raise ride height, stiffen springs, stiffen bump.
- B5 Wheel Spin on Exit: Reduce rear diff accel, soften rear spring, soften rear bump, increase rear downforce.
- B6 Lazy in Chicanes (sluggish direction change): Stiffen ARBs, stiffen rebound, stiffen springs.
